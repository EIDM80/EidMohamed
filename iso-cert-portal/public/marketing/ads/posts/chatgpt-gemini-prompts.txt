MAIN SUBJECT: a farm export manager standing among crates of fresh produce at a shipping dock, holding a rejected shipment notice, frustrated expression, farm truck behind. Shot like a real photograph — natural skin tones, realistic lighting and shadows, shallow depth of field, candid and emotionally authentic, genuine human expression that matches the dramatic headline below.

COLOR MOOD: Bright, vivid, energetic color grading built around deep vineyard purple and fresh green — rich saturated tones, warm/cool contrast, NOT dark or monochrome. The photo should feel alive, dynamic and eye-catching, like a premium modern social-media ad, not a corporate stock photo.

TEXT OVERLAY: Bold, large Arabic headline text placed over the image (top third or bottom third, whichever reads best over the photo), with a soft dark gradient scrim behind it for legibility. Right-to-left Arabic, bold modern sans-serif (Cairo Bold / Noto Kufi Arabic style). Exact headline text: "المستورد الأوروبي رفض الشحنة كاملة" — highlight the word(s) "رفض" in a bright vivid accent color (yellow or lime-green) that pops against the photo, keep the rest of the headline in white with a strong drop shadow.

SMALL LABEL: a small rounded pill tag reading "ISO · مزارع وتصدير" in white text on a semi-transparent dark background, placed near the top corner.

BOTTOM CTA: A bold, colorful, high-contrast button/pill near the bottom containing the Arabic text "اطلب الآن" and "isoorder.com", using a bright accent color (lime-green or golden yellow) that stands out sharply against the photo — should look tappable and urgent, like a real app/ad button, with a subtle glow or shadow.

Overall mood: modern, dynamic, high-energy social-media ad photography, real human emotion, rich saturated colors, dramatic lighting, attention-grabbing and "stop the scroll" style — think professional Instagram ad creative, not corporate stock photography and not a plain graphic design template.
