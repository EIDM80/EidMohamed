# Voice sample script (hand this to the user)

The voice sample builds the voice attached to every clip via Seedance's reference-audio, so it must
capture the **emotional range** the vlog uses. A flat monotone clones badly.

**How to record:** ~90 seconds, one relaxed take, natural pace (don't "perform"). Quiet room, no
music/TV, phone held a consistent distance; a soft/furnished room beats a bathroom (echo). WAV or
high-bitrate MP3, mono ok, don't shout (avoid clipping). **We then trim it to ≤24s** (Seedance's
reference-audio cap) using an expressive stretch.

Read this, letting your tone actually shift on the cues:

> *(normal, easy)* "Okay, so — I still can't totally believe where I am right now. Let me just walk
> you through it."
>
> *(bright, excited)* "Wait. Look at that. Okay that's actually insane — it's massive, and the whole
> thing is *painted*, like real color, blues and golds everywhere. I did not expect that."
>
> *(curious, thinking out loud)* "So how does this even work? There's no money here — none. People
> are trading grain, bread, jars of beer. It's the year thirteen thirty-two B.C. and I'm just...
> standing in the middle of it."
>
> *(light, a small laugh)* "And yeah — everyone's staring at me. Fair. I'm the only person here in
> jeans and sneakers. Give me two minutes, I'll fix that. Ha."
>
> *(warm, friendly)* "A guy just handed me a piece of bread and said something really kind. I don't
> know half these words yet, but honestly? I think I'm getting the hang of it."
>
> *(quiet, slow, reflective)* "And this is the part that gets me. Nobody here knows how the story
> ends. They're just... living. And three thousand years from now, we'll know all their names — and
> they have no idea. They're just here, right now. And it's kind of beautiful."

That covers normal → awe → curiosity (with a spoken date/number) → laugh → warmth → the quiet
dramatic-irony register the closers live in.

**Adapt per episode:** swap the era-specific details ("1332 B.C.", "painted", "grain, bread, beer")
to the chosen topic, but keep the emotional cues and structure.
