# Cinematic Camera Replacement — 7 حركات كاميرا سينمائية
مصدر: ملف PDF مرفوع من المستخدم (Cinematic_Camera_Replacement_1.pdf) + فيديو مرجعي (video_camera_moves.mp4، عمودي 720x1280، 84 ثانية).
مخزّن هنا للاستخدام المستقبلي في أي طلب فيديو-إلى-فيديو (Meta AI / أي أداة AI فيديو تدعم رفع فيديو مصدر + برومبت).

## الفكرة الأساسية (Camera Replacement)
تصوّر فيديو بزاوية واحدة ثابتة، وتضيف عليه حركة كاميرا سينمائية بدون إعادة تصوير وبدون معدات — الأداء (الوش، الحركة، الكلام، الليب سينك، المكان، النور) مقفول تمامًا (LOCKED)، واللي بيتغير هو الكاميرا بس (CHANGED). الإحساس المطلوب: كأن كان فيه كاميرا تانية على السيت صوّرت نفس اللحظة من مكان تاني.

## بنية البرومبت (بلوكات)
- **LOCKED** — الهوية، الحركة، الكلام/الليب سينك، المكان، النور، الجريد — يفضل زي المصدر فريم بفريم.
- **CHANGED** — الكاميرا وبس؛ لما تسيب مكانها الأصلي متترجعش له تاني.
- **EYELINE** — اتجاه النظر يفضل زي الأصلي (بيبص لنقطة فاضية بدل اللينس بعد ما الكاميرا تتحرك).
- **PHYSICS** — بارالاكس، عمق ميدان، موشن بلور واقعي.
- **MOVEMENT QUALITY** — حركة صلبة بوزن حقيقي، بدون رعشة/طفو (وصف الأثر مش اسم المعدة).
- **SHOT** — لقطة واحدة متصلة بدون قطع، الحركة لسه شغالة في آخر فريم.
- **LENS** — عدسة ثابتة بدون زوم.
- **OPENING** — الحركة بدأت قبل التسجيل (تمنع تأجيل الحركة لآخر الكليب).
- **MOTIVATION** — المعنى الدرامي للحركة مش شكلها الهندسي.
- **CAMERA** — وصف المسار بالتفصيل (فين لفين، ارتفاع، عدسة).
- **REVEAL** — إيه اللي هيدخل الكادر ومكانش موجود.
- **TIMING** — الحركة front-loaded (أسرع جزء في أول تلت، مش في النص).
- **FAILURE** — تسمية شروط الفشل بالاسم.

## 3 قواعد أساسية (لكتابة أي حركة كاميرا جديدة بنفس المنطق)
1. **متسميش المعدات أبدًا** — حتى بالنفي (لو كتبت "مش درون" بيزوّد احتمال ظهور الدرون). وصف الأثر (مسار، ارتفاع، انحناء) مش اسم الآلة.
2. **الحركة تبدأ من الفريم الأول** — قول إن الحركة بدأت قبل التسجيل، وخلي أسرع جزء في أول تلت الكليب مش في النص.
3. **خط الـ180 درجة** — خط وهمي من الشخص لاتجاه نظره؛ أي كاميرا تفضل ناحية واحدة منه. التنويع بالارتفاع/المسافة/العدسة مش بعبور الخط.

## طريقة الاستخدام

## 01 — الدوران في الاستوديو والبصة متسمّرة (Studio Detach → Orbit Behind)
**اللقطة:** حد بيتكلم قاعد/واقف، أوضة ليها عمق وراه (مترين على الأقل)، ترايبود بارتفاع العين، لقطة متوسطة، نور جانبي. يبص في العدسة من أول ثانية وميلفش وشه أبدًا. المدة: 6 ثواني.

```
Use the attached video as the performance source. This is a camera
replacement, not a re-animation.

LOCKED — must match the source frame for frame:
The subject's identity, face, facial structure, hair, beard, skin
texture, clothing, accessories and body proportions. Every body
movement, gesture, step, head turn and facial expression. The exact
timing and speed of that movement. Dialogue and lip sync. The
environment, background elements, lighting direction, time of day and
colour grade. Nothing about the performance changes — no frames added,
removed, slowed or sped up. If the subject takes four steps in the
source, they take the same four steps at the same moments here.

CHANGED — the camera only:
This shot begins exactly where the original camera was. The opening
frame matches the source framing, position, height and lens precisely.
From that point the camera departs, and the departure is permanent. Once
it has left the original position it never returns to it, never drifts
back toward it, and never reproduces that composition again at any point
in the clip. The final frame must be radically different from the first.
Remaining at or near the original framing beyond the opening frame is a
failure.

EYELINE:
This camera is purely observational — the subject does not know it is
there. Preserve their original direction of gaze at every moment; they
keep looking at whatever held their attention in the source. They must
not turn toward, acknowledge, or make eye contact with this camera.
In the source the subject is looking into the lens. That lens position
becomes an empty point in space at 12 o'clock the instant the camera
leaves it. The subject keeps addressing that empty point for the rest of
the shot, at every stage of the move, including while the camera is
behind them.

PHYSICS:
Realistic perspective, lens behaviour, parallax, depth of field, motion
blur and camera inertia for this specific placement. Foreground and
background shift at correct relative speeds. Photoreal, 24fps, filmic.

MOVEMENT QUALITY:
The camera behaves as a single rigid body with real weight. There is no
shake, no float, no sway, no bobbing, no drift and no small corrections
mid-move — every frame sits exactly on one smooth continuous path.
That path is an arc pivoting around a single fixed point on the ground.
It curves throughout and never straightens into a direct advance or a
direct retreat. Height and horizontal travel change at the same time,
never one after the other.
Speed changes smoothly with weight behind it, without jitter or stutter.
Motion blur matches the speed at every moment.
The frame shows only the subject and their location, exactly as they
appear in the source. Nothing else enters the shot — no object,
structure or person that is not already present in the source video,
including in shadows and in reflections on any surface.

SHOT:
One continuous take. No cuts, no dissolves, no freeze frames, and no
post-production speed ramping of the footage — the subject's performance
plays at exactly its original speed from first frame to last.
The camera itself does change speed, and this is intentional: it
accelerates and decelerates with real weight behind it.
The move runs across the entire duration of the clip and is still in
motion at the last frame.

LENS:
A single prime lens for the whole shot. Do not zoom. Every change in
scale comes from the camera physically travelling through space, and the
perspective distorts accordingly as it moves — near objects change size
far faster than far ones.

Clock reference: 12 o'clock is the direction the subject faces or
travels.

OPENING — the movement starts at frame one:
The camera is already in motion at the very first frame. There is no
static opening, no held frame, and no pause before the movement begins.
Treat the clip as beginning in the middle of a move that was already
under way before the recording started — the first frame is not the
beginning of the movement, only the beginning of the recording.
The opening frame matches the source framing, but it is a moving frame,
not a still one.

MOTIVATION:
The subject is speaking to someone who is not in this frame. The camera
is not illustrating the speech — it is quietly circling a person who
does not know it exists. It leaves immediately and keeps going.

CAMERA:
Camera begins at the original position at 12 o'clock, at eye level,
holding the source framing on a 35mm lens, already orbiting.
It travels continuously in one direction around the subject, passing
through 2, 3 and 4 o'clock, then behind them through 6 o'clock, and
ending at roughly 8 o'clock. It widens gradually as it travels. The
subject stays off-centre in frame throughout, never perfectly centred.
The orbit radius stays constant; height rises by a few centimetres
across the move.

REVEAL:
As the camera passes behind the subject, the space in front of them
enters frame for the first time — the room they are speaking into, and
whatever stands there. Show it plainly and let it pass; do not linger on
it or push toward it.

TIMING — the move is front-loaded:
First third — the fastest and most aggressive part of the entire move,
at full speed from the opening frame, strongest motion blur here.
Middle third — the speed carries through with momentum, still clearly
travelling.
Final third — easing down but never stopping, still in motion at the
last frame.

FAILURE — this specific shot has failed if:
Any static or near-static frame appears at the start of the clip. If the
first half-second contains no camera movement, the shot has failed.
The subject turns their head, eyes or body toward this camera at any
point, or the orbit stops, reverses, or leaves the subject facing
directly into the new lens at the end.
```

## 03 — الطلوع من الوش والرجوع ليه (Rise → Apex → Return to Face)
**اللقطة:** بصة ثابتة تحت سما مفتوحة (سطح/ميدان/ملعب/موقف/شط)، أرضية ليها شكل من فوق (بلاط/رمل/أسفلت)، تصوير وقت نور جانبي (ظل ممدود). حركة واحدة صغيرة في النص بس، بدون ميش/إيدين. متتصورش تحت أي سقف/شجرة (محتاج 10 متر فوق الراس). المدة: 8 ثواني.

```
holding the source framing on a 40mm lens, already rising.

camera went up at all.

shrinking in frame.

resembles the source composition, the camera settles or holds still at
the end, the apex is skipped or reached in the last third, or the rise
and the return read as two separate shots rather than one continuous
arc.
```

## 04 — النزول من فوق والوش بيتابع (Bird's Eye → Descend to Eye Level)
**اللقطة الأهم في التنفيذ:** الكليب يبدأ ووش الشخص مرفوع لفوق بزاوية حادة (بيبص للسما)، وطول الكليب بيزنّل راسه ببطء واستمرار، وفي آخر ثانية بيبقى بيبص عدل في العدسة. النزول لازم يكون موزّع بسرعة واحدة على الكليب كله (مش يحصل فجأة في الآخر). فوقك مفتوح تمامًا (بدون سقف/شجرة/مظلة)، والشمس ورا الموبايل مش وراك. المدة: 7 ثواني.

```
Use the attached video as the performance source. This is a camera
replacement, not a re-animation.

LOCKED — must match the source frame for frame:
The subject's identity, face, facial structure, hair, beard, skin
texture, clothing, accessories and body proportions. Every body
movement, gesture, step, head turn and facial expression. The exact
timing and speed of that movement. Dialogue and lip sync. The
environment, background elements, lighting direction, time of day and
colour grade. Nothing about the performance changes — no frames added,
removed, slowed or sped up. If the subject takes four steps in the
source, they take the same four steps at the same moments here.
In particular, the subject's head is already tilting downward across the
source clip, from looking upward at the start to level at the end. That
head movement is part of the locked performance. Reproduce its exact
angle and timing at every frame. Do not re-animate it, do not speed it
up or slow it down to suit the camera, and do not add head turns that
are not in the source.

CHANGED — the camera only:
This shot does not begin at the original camera position. Replace the
original camera entirely with the one described below, and treat it as a
second physical camera that was on set at the same moment, observing the
same action from a different position. It never drifts toward the
original framing and never reproduces the original composition at any
point, including at the end of the move.

EYELINE — the subject is aware of this camera:
Unlike an observational shot, the subject's gaze is directed at this
camera. Because their head is already tilting down through the clip,
their eyeline naturally follows the descending camera: they are looking
up at it when it is overhead and looking straight at it when it arrives
at eye level.
The camera path must be built to match the head, not the other way
round. At every frame, place the camera along the direction the head is
already facing in the source. If the head is at forty degrees up at a
given moment, the camera is at forty degrees above eye level at that
same moment.
The subject's face must remain fully visible for the entire shot. It is
never lost to the top of the head, never seen from behind, and never
fully foreshortened.

PHYSICS:
Realistic perspective, lens behaviour, parallax, depth of field, motion
blur and camera inertia for this specific placement. Foreground and
background shift at correct relative speeds. Photoreal, 24fps, filmic.
Extreme high angles foreshorten the body correctly — from directly
above, the head and shoulders dominate and the legs are hidden beneath
them.

IDENTITY GUARD:
The face is seen from a wide range of vertical angles in this shot, from
directly overhead to level. Identity must hold at every one of them.
Facial structure, proportions, hairline and features stay constant as
the viewing angle changes — they are the same face seen from a different
height, not a face being redrawn. Lip sync stays locked to the source
audio at every angle.

The camera behaves as a single rigid body with real weight. There is no
shake, no float, no sway, no bobbing, no drift and no small corrections
mid-move — every frame sits exactly on one smooth continuous path.
The path is a single descending arc. Height and horizontal travel change
at the same time, never one after the other.
Speed changes smoothly with weight behind it, without jitter or stutter.
Motion blur matches the speed at every moment.
The frame shows only the subject and their location, exactly as they
appear in the source. Nothing else enters the shot — no object,
structure or person that is not already present in the source video,
including in shadows and in reflections on any surface.

One continuous take. No cuts, no dissolves, no freeze frames, and no
post-production speed ramping of the footage — the subject's performance
plays at exactly its original speed from first frame to last.
The camera itself does change speed, and this is intentional: it
accelerates and decelerates with real weight behind it.
The move runs across the entire duration of the clip and is still in
motion at the last frame.

A single prime lens for the whole shot. Do not zoom. Every change in
scale comes from the camera physically travelling through space.

Clock reference: 12 o'clock is the direction the subject faces or
travels.

OPENING — the movement starts at frame one:
The camera is already descending at the very first frame. There is no
static opening, no held overhead frame, and no pause before the descent
begins. Treat the clip as beginning in the middle of a descent that was
already under way before the recording started — the first frame is not
the beginning of the movement, only the beginning of the recording.

MOTIVATION:
Something has come down out of the sky and stopped in front of this
person's face, and they watched it the whole way down. The camera is not
observing them — it is arriving. The descent is steady and unhurried,
the movement of something with intent.

CAMERA:
Camera begins directly above the subject, ten metres or more up,
```

## 05 — الأربع زوايا (Four-Angle Coverage — نفس ناحية خط الـ180)
**اللقطة:** أداء متصل طويل شوية (10 ثواني)، أوضة/مكان له عمق وراك وعلى جنبك. بصتك ثابتة اتجاه واحد طول الوقت (على العدسة أو نقطة ثابتة جنبها) — أي لفة راس هتخرب القطعات. أفضل طريقة تنفيذ: تولّد كل زاوية لوحدها من نفس الكليب الأصلي وتقطعهم إنت في المونتاج (استبدل بلوك SEQUENCE ببلوك زاوية واحدة + السطر: "SHOT: One continuous take, no cuts. The camera holds this position and this movement for the entire clip.").

```

replacement, not a re-animation.

LOCKED — must match the source frame for frame:
The subject's identity, face, facial structure, hair, beard, skin

environment, background elements, lighting direction, time of day and
colour grade. Nothing about the performance changes — no frames added,
removed, slowed or sped up. If the subject takes four steps in the
source, they take the same four steps at the same moments here.
The performance runs continuously across every angle: when the sequence
cuts, it cuts to the same instant of the same performance seen from
elsewhere, never to a different moment and never to a repeated one.

CHANGED — the cameras only:
Replace the original camera with the four cameras described below. Treat
them as four physical cameras that were all on set at the same moment,
all recording the same continuous action from different positions. None
of them ever reproduces the original composition.

THE LINE — the single most important constraint in this shot:
Establish an imaginary straight line running through the subject in the
direction they are looking in the source. All four cameras sit on the
same side of that line for the entire sequence. No camera crosses it, at
any point, for any reason.
The consequence must hold in every frame: the subject faces the same
side of the screen in all four angles. If they look toward the left of
frame in the first angle, they look toward the left of frame in all
four. Their gaze direction never flips between angles.
Variation comes from height, distance and lens, never from crossing to
the other side.

EYELINE:
These cameras are purely observational — the subject does not know they
are there. Preserve their original direction of gaze at every moment;
they keep looking at whatever held their attention in the source. They
must not turn toward, acknowledge, or make eye contact with any of these

Realistic perspective, lens behaviour, parallax, depth of field, motion

background shift at correct relative speeds. Photoreal, 24fps, filmic.

Each camera behaves as a single rigid body with real weight. There is no

constant speed. The static angles are locked off completely, with no
creeping push and no drift whatsoever.
The frame shows only the subject and their location, exactly as they

structure or person that is not already present in the source video,
including in shadows and in reflections on any surface.

SEQUENCE:
This is a four-angle edited sequence, not a single take. There are
exactly three hard cuts, dividing the clip into four roughly equal
parts. Every cut is an instant hard cut — no dissolves, no fades, no
whips, no transitions of any kind.
The audio and the performance run continuously straight through every
cut with no break, no repeat and no gap.
Angle one begins in motion at the very first frame. There is no static
opening and no held frame before the sequence starts.

LENS:
No zooming within any angle. Each angle holds one focal length for its
whole duration. Scale changes only between angles, never inside one.

Clock reference: 12 o'clock is the direction the subject is looking.
All four cameras sit between 12 and 6 o'clock, moving clockwise. None of
them is ever placed between 6 and 12 o'clock.

ANGLE 1 — wide, eye level, moving:
A wide shot at 1 o'clock, at eye level, on a 24mm lens, showing the
subject full length with the space around them. The camera slides
sideways along a straight horizontal path at a slow constant speed,
travelling from 1 o'clock toward 2 o'clock, so the background shifts
steadily behind the subject with strong parallax. It is already moving
at the first frame and still moving when the cut arrives.

ANGLE 2 — medium, low, static:
A medium shot at 4 o'clock, positioned low, below chest height, looking
slightly upward at the subject on a 35mm lens, framing them from the
waist up. The camera is completely locked off — no movement of any kind.
The low position makes the subject tower slightly and brings the ceiling
or sky into the top of frame.

ANGLE 3 — close, eye level, moving:
A close shot at 2 o'clock, at eye level, on a 50mm lens, framing head
and shoulders with a shallow depth of field and the background thrown
out of focus. The camera slides sideways along a straight horizontal
path at a slow constant speed, travelling in the opposite direction to
angle one, from 2 o'clock back toward 1 o'clock. Still moving when the
cut arrives.

ANGLE 4 — profile, slightly high, static:
A near-profile shot at 5 o'clock, positioned slightly above eye level,
on a 40mm lens, framing the subject from the chest up with the room
falling away behind them. The camera is completely locked off. This is
the final angle and it holds to the last frame of the clip.

CUTS:
Every cut changes both the angle and the shot size at the same time. No
two consecutive angles share a similar framing or a similar height —
wide to medium, medium to close, close to profile.
No cut lands in the middle of a large gesture or a head movement. Cuts
fall on the steadier moments between them.

FAILURE — this sequence has failed if:
Any camera crosses to the far side of the line, or the subject appears
to face a different side of the screen after a cut.
Any static or near-static frame appears at the start of the clip.
The cuts are dissolves, fades or transitions rather than instant hard
cuts.
The performance repeats, skips or resets at a cut instead of running
continuously through it.
Two consecutive angles share the same shot size or the same height.
The angles that should be locked off drift or creep, or the sliding
angles change speed mid-move.
Lighting direction or colour grade changes between angles.
```

## 06 — كل حاجة طايرة إلا هو (Suspended World → Full 360 Return)
**أثقل برومبت في الملف.** الشرط الأهم: لازم حواليك حاجات سايبة (ورق، أوراق شجر، رمل، زلط، كوباية، كراسي، شنط، كابلات، تراب) — مكان فاضي نضيف = مفيش شوت. ترايبود بارتفاع العين، لقطة متوسطة واسعة (لازم تبان الأرض حواليك). واقف ثابت تمامًا، متلمسش حاجة ومتحركش رجلك. المدة: 8 ثواني.
لو طلع مضروب، رتّب التنازلات: (1) اقلل التوزيع بالعمق وخليهم قريبين منك، (2) انزل من 360° لـ180° وخليها تقف ورا ضهرك، (3) بلوك GRAVITY متتنازلش عنه أبدًا — هو نفسه الشوت.

```
Use the attached video as the performance source. The subject is a
camera-replacement element and must be preserved exactly. The
environment around them is re-animated as described below.

LOCKED — the subject only, must match the source frame for frame:
The subject's identity, face, facial structure, hair, beard, skin
texture, clothing, accessories and body proportions. Every body
movement, gesture, step, head turn and facial expression. The exact
timing and speed of that movement. Dialogue and lip sync. Nothing about
their performance changes — no frames added, removed, slowed or sped up.
They play at normal real-time speed throughout, never in slow motion.
Also locked: the location itself, its architecture and surfaces, the
lighting direction, the time of day and the colour grade. These are the
same room, the same light and the same grade as the source.

CHANGED — the camera, and the loose objects only:
Replace the original camera with the one described below, and suspend
the loose objects in the scene as described below. Nothing else about
the environment is altered. No object is added that is not already
present in the source video, and no object is removed.

SUSPENSION — how the world behaves:
Every loose, unattached object visible in the source scene has lost its
weight. Papers, leaves, dust, grit, sand, small debris, cups, bags,
cables, light furniture — anything not fixed to the building — has
lifted off the ground and hangs in the air around the subject.
They rise slowly and drift almost imperceptibly, as if time is running
at a fraction of normal speed for them alone. They rotate very slowly on
their own axes. They never fall, never accelerate downward, and never
come to rest.
They are distributed through the depth of the scene — some close to the
lens, some near the subject, some far behind — so the orbit reveals them
at different distances and they pass through the frame at different
apparent speeds.
Objects fixed to the world stay fixed: floors, walls, ceilings,
doorways, trees, parked vehicles, anything structural.

GRAVITY — the subject is the exception:
The subject alone remains bound by gravity. Their feet stay in contact
with the ground for the entire shot. They never lift, never float, never
drift. Their hair and clothing hang and move under normal gravity at
normal speed, never suspended and never in slow motion.
They do not react to the floating objects, do not look at them, and do
not touch them. They behave exactly as they do in the source, as though
nothing unusual is happening.
This contrast is the entire shot: a world in suspension and one person
still living at normal speed inside it.

SHADOWS:
Objects that have left the ground lose their contact shadows and cast
shadows onto the surfaces below them instead, consistent with the
lighting direction in the source. The subject keeps their grounded
contact shadow at their feet throughout.

EYELINE:
This camera is purely observational — the subject does not know it is
there. Preserve their original direction of gaze at every moment; they
keep looking at whatever held their attention in the source. In the
source they are looking into the lens; that lens position becomes an
empty point in space at 12 o'clock the instant the camera leaves it, and
they keep addressing that empty point for the entire orbit, including
while the camera is behind them.

Realistic perspective, lens behaviour, parallax, depth of field, motion
blur and camera inertia for this specific placement. Foreground and
background shift at correct relative speeds. Photoreal, 24fps, filmic.
Suspended objects show almost no motion blur of their own because they
are barely moving, but they do streak across frame from the camera's
movement. The subject shows normal motion blur for normal-speed
movement.

MOVEMENT QUALITY:
The camera behaves as a single rigid body with real weight. There is no
shake, no float, no sway, no bobbing, no drift and no small corrections
mid-move — every frame sits exactly on one smooth continuous circle.
The orbit radius stays constant and the subject stays at a constant size
in frame while the camera travels.
The frame shows only the subject and their location as they appear in
the source, with the suspension described above. Nothing else enters the
shot — no object, structure or person not already present in the source
video, including in shadows and in reflections on any surface.

SHOT:
One continuous take. No cuts, no dissolves, no freeze frames, and no
post-production speed ramping of the footage. The subject's performance
plays at exactly its original speed from first frame to last.
The move runs across the entire duration of the clip and is still in
motion at the last frame.

LENS:
A single prime lens for the whole shot. Do not zoom.

Clock reference: 12 o'clock is the direction the subject faces.

OPENING — the movement starts at frame one:
The camera is already orbiting at the very first frame, and the objects
are already off the ground and already hanging. There is no static
opening, no held frame, and no moment where the world is normal before
the effect begins. Treat the clip as beginning in the middle of
something that was already under way before the recording started.

MOTIVATION:
A single moment has been stretched open and the camera is walking around
inside it. The orbit is fast and continuous, the world is nearly frozen,
and the person at the centre is the only thing still moving at the speed
of life.

CAMERA:
Camera begins close on the subject's face at 12 o'clock, at eye level,
on a 35mm lens, already orbiting.
It travels a complete 360 degree circle around the subject in one
unbroken movement, passing through 3, 6 and 9 o'clock and returning to
12, holding the subject at a constant size and near the centre of frame
throughout. It tilts to keep them framed as it goes.
It returns to the face at 12 o'clock at the end, but arrives closer to
the subject and lower than it began, so the final frame is a tighter,
lower angle and not a repeat of the opening one.
It is still orbiting and still closing at the final frame — it never
settles, never locks off, and never comes to rest.

REVEAL:
The orbit uncovers the full extent of the suspension. Objects the
opening frame could not show — the ones behind the subject, above them,
and out to the sides — arrive gradually as the camera comes around, so
the scale of what has happened is only fully understood by the time the
circle closes.

TIMING — the move is front-loaded:
First third — the fastest part of the orbit, at full speed from the
opening frame, sweeping from 12 through 3 o'clock, strongest streaking
of foreground objects here.
Middle third — the speed carries through with momentum, passing behind
the subject through 6 o'clock.
Final third — easing but never stopping, coming around through 9 and
back to 12, still moving and still closing at the last frame.

FAILURE — this specific shot has failed if:
Any static or near-static frame appears at the start of the clip, or the
objects are on the ground at the opening frame and lift later.
The subject floats, drifts, lifts off the ground, or moves in slow
motion.
The subject looks at, reacts to, or touches the floating objects.
Objects fall, settle, or accelerate downward at any point.
Objects appear that are not present in the source video.
The orbit stops short of a full circle, reverses, pauses, or the final
frame repeats the opening framing.
Structural parts of the location lift off with the loose objects.
```

## 07 — الطيران (Break Away → FPV Pass)
**اللقطة:** أوسع لقطة في الملف — مساحة كبيرة مفتوحة، ويفضّل يكون فيها حاجات تعدي جنب العدسة (أعمدة، عربيات، شجر، كراسي) عشان الإحساس بالسرعة يبان — فراغ تام بدون مرجع بيقتل الإحساس بالسرعة. ترايبود بارتفاع العين، لقطة واسعة (جسمك كله). أداء أكبر من باقي الشوتات (إيدين، مشي، لفة). المدة: 6 ثواني فقط. ملاحظة: لو طلع فيه اختراع مكان (مباني/أرض مش موجودة)، قلّل الارتفاع في بلوك CAMERA وخليها تحت مستوى الراس بدل ما تطلع.

```

The camera is a small, light, fast-moving body flying freely through the

Banking and turning are the same action: the camera cannot change
direction without rolling into the turn first. Every change of heading

stylistic angle — it is always the by-product of a turn.

the ground, and no reflection of it on any surface. Only the world it
flies through is seen.

One very wide lens, in the region of 14mm to 16mm, for the whole shot.

outward. Do not zoom.
Deep focus throughout — everything from the nearest foreground object to
the far background stays sharp. There is no shallow depth of field, no
background blur and no focus racking at any point.

The flight path passes close to objects that already exist in the source

speed. There is no static opening, no hover, no held frame, and no pause
before the movement begins. Treat the clip as beginning in the middle of
a flight that was already under way before the recording started.

Something small and fast is loose in this space and the subject is one
of the things it flies past. The camera is not observing them
respectfully — it is moving through, and they happen to be in the way.

through 3 o'clock and out to 5 o'clock at height.

Final third — the dive, the level-out and the close pass, still
accelerating away at the last frame.
```
